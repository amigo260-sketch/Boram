30+ Years of Trust and Expertise

Built on decades of experience in the non-ferrous metal import and supply industry, delivering consistent quality and reliable supply chains.

Boram Trading Co. was established on August 1, 1998 as a specialized non-ferrous metal trading company.

We import raw materials including copper, aluminum, magnesium, and zinc, along with primary processed products such as oxygen-free copper billets, brass products, and copper master alloys from overseas suppliers.

With over 30 years of experience, we have built a stable supply chain and trusted partnerships with clients across Korea.
